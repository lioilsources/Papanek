Papánek is an offline cookbook for beginners. It knows what you have at home and picks a recipe.

• Add ingredients to your pantry; salt, pepper, oil and other basics are assumed
• See what you can cook right now, what is missing one or two things and what needs shopping
• Step-by-step cooking mode with timers
• Missing ingredients go to your shopping list in one tap, and bought items move to your pantry

Recipes are currently in Czech and English; the app interface is available in 45 languages.
