Frequently Asked Questions

I bought a cuisine pack on a new phone and it is locked.

Open the Cuisines tab and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

My pantry and shopping list disappeared.

They are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them; there is no cloud backup.

Does Papánek need an internet connection?

No. Recipes, pictures, the pantry and the shopping list all work offline. Only purchases need a connection; anonymous usage statistics are sent when you are online and wait on the device when you are not.

Privacy

Papánek collects no personal data. It sends anonymous usage statistics to the developer's own server, which you can turn off in the settings. See the Privacy Policy.
